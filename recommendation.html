Why this setup works

  • Built-in lighting makes indoor growing straightforward.
  • Twelve pod sites provide room for a useful mix of herbs and greens.
  • The compact reservoir and pump require little assembly.

Not ideal for

Full-sized tomatoes, cucumbers, chillies or other large fruiting crops. These need deeper root space and stronger structural support.
